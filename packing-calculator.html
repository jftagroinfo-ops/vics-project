What Makes a Packing Plan Reliable?

A container's cubic capacity does not mean it can carry that volume at any weight. Heavy commodities usually reach a payload or route limit before filling a 40ft container; low-density products may reach volume first. High Cube equipment adds space, not unlimited legal payload.

For final planning, replace averages with an assigned container number and CSC plate, carrier booking restrictions, measured gross bag weight, filled-bag dimensions, pallet layout and loading trial. The verified gross mass must follow the applicable shipping process.
