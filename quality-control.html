Our 4-Stage Traceability

  • Farm & Mandi Procurement

    We source directly from trusted farmer networks and major APMC mandis, ensuring crop freshness and authentic origin.

  • Sortex & Processing

    Cleaning and optical sorting reduce foreign matter and discoloured grains against the contracted grade limits.

  • Hygienic Packaging

    Approved food-contact packaging, calibrated weighing and sealing checks are applied to the selected pack format.

  • Container Stuffing

    Containers are checked for cleanliness and suitability, prepared with kraft paper or desiccants when required, then sealed and recorded.
