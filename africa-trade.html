Compliance

Africa Import Compliance

Importer and product authorization

The buyer identifies the importer of record and confirms product registration, import permit, licence, label language and shelf-life rules with the destination authority.

Inspection programme

Any pre-export verification, conformity assessment or destination inspection must name the current programme, agency, scope, booking party and certificate before loading.

Plant-health and treatment

The importer confirms import-permit conditions and phytosanitary wording. Fumigation or other treatment is used only when applicable and permitted, with method and evidence written into the contract.

Origin and shipping documents

The commercial invoice, packing list, transport document and any certificate of origin follow the transaction. Issuer, legalization and destination-specific documents are confirmed rather than assumed.

Tariff and tax check

The importer or customs broker verifies the current classification, customs value, duty, levy, tax and any concession for the exact product and origin before contracting.

Transit and delivery plan

For landlocked delivery, name the transit country, border posts, bond or guarantee, clearing agents, inland Incoterm point and allocation of delay, storage and demurrage risk.
