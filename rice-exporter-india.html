Overview

What JFT Agro Supplies

JFT Agro Overseas exports 20 distinct Indian rice grades spanning Basmati (1121, 1401, 1509 and 1718, in Golden Sella, Steam, Raw and White Sella forms), parboiled IR-64 in 5% and 10% broken grades, Silky Sortex white and short-grain rice in 5% and 25% broken grades, 100% broken rice, Samba rice and PR-11 rice. Each grade is a separate product page with its own specification table -- length, moisture, broken percentage, purity -- because buyers sourcing rice typically need one specific grade, not a general rice inquiry.

Selection

Specification & Selection Considerations

Rice grades are distinguished primarily by variety (Basmati vs. non-Basmati), broken percentage, and processing (raw, steam, parboiled, or sella/golden sella). The correct grade depends on the buyer's destination market and intended use -- Basmati grades serve premium retail and foodservice markets, while IR-64 and broken-rice grades serve bulk food-security and processing markets. Every product page states the reference HS code, MOQ and packing options; the signed specification and an approved sample control the actual order.

Rice from this catalogue currently ships to UAE, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran and West African markets including Benin, among others -- see each product's own page for its specific documented markets.
