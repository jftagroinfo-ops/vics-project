Buyer security

Secure Your Purchase

Use this sequence before paying for any order. Each certificate, specification, inspection, bank instruction and shipment document must match the named legal entity and the signed contract.

Independently verify beneficiary bank details

Before every remittance, confirm the beneficiary name, account number and bank instructions using a previously verified telephone number or contact channel. Do not accept a last-minute bank change by email or WhatsApp alone. JFT Agro will not ask a buyer to remit to an unrelated personal account.

Controlled buyer journey

From due diligence to document release

1. Verify the legal entity

Match the contracting name, registered address, tax/export registrations and authorised signatory to current original documents.

2. Approve product and sample

Record grade, tolerances, crop/origin, packing, label, destination requirements and the approved reference sample.

3. Agree the sales contract

Confirm quantity, price, currency, Incoterm, port, shipment window, inspection, document list, claims process and cancellation terms.

4. Verify payment instructions

Use bank details printed on the signed commercial document and reconfirm them independently before sending funds.

5. Inspect and prepare

Production, packing evidence and any independent inspection or laboratory scope follow the written contract—not a generic website promise.

6. Match shipment documents

Check commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, origin and product/destination certificates against the contract before release.

Buyer verification checklist

  • Legal entity and registered address
  • Certificate holder, number, scope and expiry
  • Beneficiary name matches the contract
  • Approved product specification and tolerances
  • Incoterm and named port/place
  • Inspection party and responsibility
  • Required destination documents
  • Shipment and document-release conditions
  • Written complaint and claims procedure
  • Known-contact confirmation of bank changes

Begin with a verifiable requirement

Send the product, quantity, packing, destination and shipment month. Request current evidence before contracting.
