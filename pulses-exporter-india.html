What JFT Agro Supplies

JFT Agro Overseas exports 4 Indian pulse products: yellow peas (matar), chickpeas (kabuli), green mung beans and toor dal (split pigeon pea). Pulse buyers typically specify by count-per-100g or size grading, moisture, foreign matter, and split/whole form -- each product page states the applicable reference specification.
