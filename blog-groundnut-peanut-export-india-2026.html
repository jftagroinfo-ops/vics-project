Importing Groundnuts from India: Grades, Testing and Shipment Controls

Updated 12 August 2026 8 min read JFT Agro Trade Desk

A successful peanut contract starts with more than a count size and a price. Buyers should define variety, moisture, damaged kernels, aflatoxin limits, microbiological requirements, packing and the exact inspection method before approving a shipment.

Java, Bold and blanched peanuts

Indian export offers commonly distinguish Java kernels, which are smaller and rounder, from larger Bold kernels. Blanched peanuts have the skin removed and require a separate process specification. The right selection depends on whether the cargo will be roasted, crushed, coated, used in confectionery or sold as a retail snack.

Count descriptions such as 40/50 or 50/60 usually indicate the approximate number of kernels per ounce. The contract should state the counting method and permitted tolerance because commercial descriptions can differ between suppliers and destinations.

Treat aflatoxin as a contract item

Aflatoxin risk cannot be assessed reliably from appearance alone. Buyers should identify the destination limit, approved sampling plan, accredited laboratory and point of testing. A composite sample drawn from representative bags or lots is more meaningful than a convenient sample taken from the top of one bag.

APEDA operates Peanut.NET to support testing, certification and traceability for peanut exports from India. The buyer should still verify the certificate scope, batch identity and destination requirements before shipment.

Pre-shipment buyer checklist

  • Approve a sealed reference sample and keep a counter-sample.
  • Write the laboratory limits and test method into the purchase contract.
  • Confirm crop year, origin, count, moisture and defect tolerances.
  • Inspect bag condition, container cleanliness and desiccant plan.
  • Match lot numbers across packing list, test report and inspection record.

Packing and container planning

Common bulk options include food-grade polypropylene or jute bags with an inner liner. Retail and private-label packs require artwork approval, net-weight controls, carton strength and destination labelling checks. The selected pack should protect kernels from moisture while allowing the buyer's warehouse and distribution process to work efficiently.

Container loading should be documented with photographs showing the empty container, floor and wall condition, bag arrangement, seal application and final door closure. If independent inspection is required, name its scope in the contract; “inspection included” is too vague.

How to compare two peanut quotations

Normalize both offers to the same Incoterm, port, count, crop year, specification and packing. A lower headline price can become more expensive after adding inspection, testing, stronger packing or destination charges. Ask each exporter to state exclusions and offer validity clearly.

Important

Food-safety limits and import controls can change. Confirm current requirements with your importer, competent authority and laboratory before contracting. This guide is procurement information, not regulatory advice.
