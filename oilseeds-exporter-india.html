Overview

What JFT Agro Supplies

JFT Agro Overseas exports 5 Indian oilseed products: sesame seeds (natural and hulled), groundnuts (peanuts), soya bean, sunflower seeds (in shell) and black cumin seeds (nigella). Oilseed buyers generally split into two groups -- crushing/processing buyers focused on oil content and yield, and food/confectionery buyers focused on size grading, colour and cleanliness -- and the correct product page states which specification applies.

Selection

Specification & Selection Considerations

Oil content, foreign-matter percentage, moisture and admixture are the primary specification fields for crushing buyers; size and colour grading matter more for food-use sesame and confectionery-grade groundnuts. 5 distinct HS codes apply across this group.

This group's most frequently documented markets are the USA, Europe, Vietnam, the Middle East and China.
