# SEO Implementation Phase 13 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 57–61: the groundnut buyer guide, sunflower seeds, safflower seeds, whole soybeans and soybean meal DOC.

## Implemented work

### Groundnut buyer guide

- Added the revised 2025 Codex peanut aflatoxin code as the primary prevention, sampling and shipment-status source.
- Retained APEDA Peanut.NET as the India-specific testing, certification and traceability reference while clarifying that registration or another lot's report does not prove the cargo.
- Reframed Java and Bold as commercial labels requiring measured count, defects and approved-sample evidence.
- Added ready-to-eat versus further-processing status and a shipment-release evidence matrix.
- Replaced generic packing availability with bag, liner, artwork, legal-label and line-capability approval fields.
- Updated visible and Article structured-data modification dates to 20 August 2026.

### Sunflower seeds

- Separated whole in-shell sunflower seed from dehulled kernel availability and specifications.
- Added intended food, feed, bird-food or crushing use instead of treating “bird feed grade” as a universal product identity.
- Added size basis, empty and damaged seed, residual hull, process status, composition, safety and lot-evidence fields.
- Clarified that commodity supply does not imply certified planting seed.

### Safflower seeds

- Added *Carthamus tinctorius* identity supported by ICAR and required crop/region evidence for the offered lot.
- Removed the unsupported assumption that every lot originates in Maharashtra.
- Separated whole seed, partially decorticated meal and hull identities.
- Added distinct crushing, food and feed enquiry requirements without claiming universal suitability or oil yield.

### Soybeans

- Standardized visible naming on “soybean,” retaining “soya bean” as a natural supporting synonym and preserving the existing URL/canonical.
- Separated whole beans from expeller, meal, dehulled meal and hulls.
- Added intended food, feed or crushing use, raw/processed status and soybean-allergen planning.
- Replaced origin-implied non-GMO assumptions with destination rule, claim wording, segregation, sampling, event scope and method requirements.

### Soybean meal DOC

- Clarified that DOC is market shorthand and does not establish extracted-meal, dehulled, expeller or heat-treatment identity.
- Removed unverified 46–48% protein and other fixed composition claims.
- Required crude-protein minimum/range, analytical method and as-received or dry-matter reporting basis.
- Added extraction, dehulling, heat-treatment, urease or other process indicator, GMO evidence, feed-safety sampling and traceability fields.
- Connected the product to whole soybean, COA, purchase-specification and sample workflows.

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee form availability, identity, origin, intended-use acceptance, count, grade, composition, protein, oil yield, dehulling, extraction, heat treatment, GMO status, allergen control, aflatoxin or other safety results, packing approval, document availability or destination compliance. Those outcomes depend on the signed specification, current law, representative sampling, named methods, lot-linked evidence and buyer verification.
