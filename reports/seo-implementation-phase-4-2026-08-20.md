# SEO Implementation Phase 4 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 17 and 18 by turning the UAE and Europe market pages into source-backed importer planning hubs rather than broad destination-sales pages.

## UAE trade hub

- Replaced broad certification and direct-supply snippet claims with an importer-focused title and description.
- Clarified that the UAE importer and broker confirm the applicable product registration, label, permit and clearance route.
- Added current official references for UAE national food registration, Dubai Municipality food services and UAE–India CEPA.
- Preserved CEPA as a tariff-line and origin-rule check; no preferential treatment is assumed.
- Removed unsupported port-demand, shipment-frequency and buyer-preference statements.
- Added contextual paths to the documentation center, purchase-specification guide, COA guide and container-loading checklist.

## EU and UK trade hub

- Changed the page intent from mixed “Europe and CIS” coverage to EU and UK compliance planning.
- Replaced broad “EU-compliant” and certified-supplier snippet claims with buyer-verifiable planning language.
- Explained that Great Britain and EU MRL regimes must be checked separately; EU rules continue to apply in Northern Ireland.
- Linked directly to the European Commission MRL material and the Great Britain HSE MRL register.
- Added the European Commission imported-product controls source and HSE import-tolerance overview.
- Removed unsupported port-demand and market-preference statements.
- Added contextual paths to export documentation, quality control, COA review and the existing UK/EU spice buyer checklist.
