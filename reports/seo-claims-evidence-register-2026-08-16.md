## Status legend

- **Publish with evidence**: keep the claim only when the current document can be supplied to a buyer and its holder/scope match the contracting entity.
- **Contract-qualified**: acceptable when the page clearly says availability, testing, capacity or documentation is confirmed for the specific contract.
- **Needs owner proof**: do not strengthen or add the claim elsewhere until evidence is filed.

## Publication rule

For each document, record: legal holder, registration/certificate number, issuing authority, scope, issue date, expiry date, verification URL and the pages where it is claimed. If a document expires or does not match the contracting entity, change the public wording to “available when included in the contract” or remove it until renewed.

Do not publish certificate numbers, signatures, QR codes, bank details or complete certificates without redacting information that could enable impersonation or fraud. Supply full copies only through controlled buyer due diligence.
