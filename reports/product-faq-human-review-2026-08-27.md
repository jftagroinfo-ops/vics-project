## Part O — Arabic "at sight" terminology

**Phase 6 finding:** the retired payment-terms FAQ answer read
`"We accept LC في الافق، TT (T / T)، وDP"` — "في الافق" translates literally to
"on the horizon," which reads as a mistranslation of the trade term "at sight"
(as in "LC at sight": payment due immediately on presentation of compliant
documents, as opposed to a usance/deferred LC).

**Resolution this phase:** the current English product FAQ (the 5-question,
data-driven design generated by `product_faq()`) **does not include a
payment-terms question at all** — it was dropped when the FAQ was redesigned,
confirmed by direct inspection of the current English source (zero matches for
"LC", "TT", "at sight", or "payment" in any of the 5 current question/answer
templates). Regenerating the locale FAQ to match the current English design
therefore **removes the disputed phrase from every page it appeared on** —
verified: 0 occurrences of "في الافق" remain sitewide after this phase's fix
(previously present wherever the old payment-terms answer was translated).

This is a **removal, not a correction** — the phrase is not carried forward in
any form, correct or otherwise. Per this phase's own instruction ("do not
automatically replace it... if automated verification cannot establish
correctness, mark HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED"): no replacement was written, so there
is nothing to mark HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED for this specific phrase going
forward. For the record, since Part O asks for the intended meaning to be
documented regardless: "at sight" in trade finance means the issuing bank pays
the exporter immediately upon presentation of complying documents, rather than
on a deferred/usance basis. The standard Arabic banking term for this is
"عند الاطلاع" (literally "upon sight/viewing"), which is the term a native
reviewer would likely use if a payment-terms FAQ is reintroduced in a future
phase. **This is a documentation note for that future decision, not a live
translation this phase is publishing.**
