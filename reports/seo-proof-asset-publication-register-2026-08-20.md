## Publication gate

A future redacted proof asset may be published only after the trade/compliance owner confirms all of the following:

1. The file was derived from the current original rather than a badge, screenshot or expired copy.
2. The holder's legal name matches the contracting entity.
3. The issuer and document type are visible.
4. The covered site, activity, product or certification scope remains readable.
5. Issue, expiry or current-validity fields remain readable.
6. An official registry or issuer verification route is recorded where available.
7. Redaction does not remove so much information that the copy becomes unverifiable.
8. Personal data, signatures, banking information and confidential identifiers are reviewed before publication.
9. The public asset includes a redaction notice, last-reviewed date, accessible alt text and a version-controlled filename.
10. The HTML description and structured data state only what the published evidence supports.

## Required filename pattern

`proof-{issuer}-{document-type}-{holder-slug}-{review-date}-redacted-v{n}.{ext}`

Example pattern only: `proof-issuer-rcmc-legal-entity-2026-08-20-redacted-v1.pdf`

## Owner action required

The first proof asset cannot be published from repository material alone. A current original document, authority to publish a redacted copy and a completed holder/scope/validity review are required.
