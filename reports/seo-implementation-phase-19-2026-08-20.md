# SEO Implementation Phase 19 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 87–91: Mundra rice logistics, Kandla agricultural logistics, Kakinada IR64 logistics, the India agro-export documentation centre and the purchase-specification template.

The Mundra page had enough current port, terminal and customs evidence to justify a distinct operational guide. Kandla and Kakinada remain unpublished because port capability does not establish a serviceable JFT route. The two existing buyer resources were expanded, and every validated commercial cluster now links contextually to the documentation centre.

## Implemented work

### Mundra rice logistics

- Created an English, self-canonical page at `/logistics/mundra-rice-exports/`.
- Separated general Mundra capability from shipment-specific product, destination, carrier and JFT acceptance.
- Added seven controls covering rice policy/admissibility, measurable specification, terminal booking, documents, lot/container release, customs/gate milestones and final buyer handover.
- Added explicit warnings that Mundra is not one terminal instruction and that gate, VGM, documentation, customs and carrier cut-offs are separate.
- Added a comparable-offer matrix plus crawlable Basmati, IR64, documentation, loading, transit, JNPA and specification paths.
- Added official Mundra terminal/connectivity, downloads, live-schedule, ICEGATE and CBIC eSANCHIT sources checked on 20 August 2026.
- Linked the page from the port-transit calculator, JNPA logistics guide, 1121 Basmati and IR64 product routes, and added it to the sitemap.

### Kandla logistics publication gate

- Did not create `/logistics/kandla-agro-exports/`.

### Kakinada IR64 publication gate

### Agro export documentation centre

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee product exportability, destination import permission, port/terminal acceptance, container equipment, carrier space, cut-off, customs release, vessel loading, transit time, free time, tariff or route serviceability. Those outcomes require current policy, importer evidence, an actual booking, a named terminal and shipment-specific operational approval.
