# SEO Implementation Phase 9 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 37–41: corn gluten meal, cumin seeds, the 2026 cumin market outlook, coriander seeds and the Eagle-versus-Scooter coriander guide.

## Implemented work

### Corn gluten meal

- Distinguished the starch-manufacturing feed residue from corn starch, maize flour and food ingredients.
- Replaced generic premium, fixed-purity, certification, port and universal-document language with buyer-defined feed specifications.
- Added protein-basis, contaminants, intended species/use, destination approval, packing and formulation-responsibility fields.
- Connected the page to current DGFT classification, the EU feed-material catalogue, processed-maize alternatives and the purchase-specification workflow.

### Cumin seeds

- Reframed FAQ, Singapore quality, bold and European quality as commercial descriptions rather than universal grades.
- Added measurable identity, visual, composition, residue, microbiology, sample and lot-release fields.
- Replaced unconditional document, application and packing claims with contract- and destination-specific language.
- Connected the product page to the market guide, coriander cluster, specification guide and sample request path.

### Cumin price outlook

- Removed unsupported historical prices, crop estimates, exact FOB/freight benchmarks, transit times, buyer-volume claims and contracting recommendations.
- Added a source-labeled 1 August 2026 Spices Board market snapshot for Chennai, Jodhpur, Delhi and Unjha with the report's non-contractual limitation.
- Added a repeatable refresh workflow using Spices Board and the Government of India AGMARKNET dataset.
- Explained why mandi prices are not FOB/CFR/CIF quotes and supplied a formula-based export-cost structure without inventing figures.
- Added grade-name, destination-MRL, lot-specific COA, RFQ and commercial-risk controls.

### Coriander seeds and Eagle/Scooter guide

- Positioned Eagle and Scooter as supplier-specific trade labels unless measurable limits and samples are supplied.
- Added a direct comparison table showing what cannot be verified from either name and the contract evidence required.
- Added FSSAI, Spices Board, destination-test, sealed-sample and inspection guidance.
- Connected the product and editorial pages to each other, the spice-import guide, purchase-specification guide and sample workflow.

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee feed suitability, food compliance, grade identity, residue acceptance, document availability, price direction, freight, transit time or destination approval. Those outcomes depend on the written contract, current law, representative lot, named test methods, shipment route and buyer verification.
