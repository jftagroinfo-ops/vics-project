# JFT Agro Buyer Content Strategy

**Prepared:** 16 August 2026  
**Scope:** English buyer education, organic-search acquisition and conversion support

## Executive conclusion

JFT Agro now has 37 English insight articles. The strongest existing coverage explains commodities, export markets, trade documents and payment risk. The largest useful gap was operational content that helps an importer make a safer purchasing decision. The first new cluster therefore addresses specification writing, laboratory evidence and container loading—the connected workflow from RFQ to shipment release.

Publishing more pages alone will not produce durable rankings. The sustainable approach is a smaller cadence of original, source-backed guides that answer a real procurement question, link to relevant products and controls, disclose their review date, and are refreshed when rules or market conditions change.

## Content and search-intent audit

### Strengths

- Broad product and destination coverage already creates topical relevance for Indian rice, spices, pulses, grains and oilseeds.
- Existing trade-document, payment and buyer-security articles support trust near the enquiry stage.
- Product, infrastructure and quality-control pages provide natural internal-link destinations.
- Article pages use canonical URLs, descriptive titles, meta descriptions, breadcrumb markup and Article/FAQ structured data where appropriate.

### Priority gaps found

1. **Operational procurement templates:** buyers need measurable RFQs, inspection checklists and release criteria—not general export promotion.
2. **Evidence interpretation:** importers search for help assessing COAs, laboratory scope, sampling and lot identity.
3. **Pre-shipment risk controls:** container suitability, loading evidence, seal control and VGM affect arrival quality.
4. **Destination-specific compliance:** future pages should address a single market and product only after checking the current competent-authority source.
5. **Packaging decisions:** BOPP artwork approval, label data, pallet/wood controls and condensation planning can connect information searches to qualified enquiries.

## Editorial standard for every article

- Start with one buyer problem and one primary search intent.
- Use a unique title/H1, concise meta description and readable URL.
- Put the decision answer near the top; remove generic filler.
- Cite primary sources for regulatory, testing and safety claims.
- Separate legal requirements, contract choices and JFT Agro process statements.
- Include measurable checklists, tables or decision steps where useful.
- Add descriptive image alt text and an original, relevant hero visual.
- Link to two or more relevant internal resources and one clear next action.
- Add Article and Breadcrumb schema; use FAQ schema only for genuine visible FAQs.
- Display author/reviewer, publication/review date and a correction channel.

## Maintenance schedule

- **Regulatory/destination articles:** review quarterly and whenever a competent authority changes a rule.
- **Market/data articles:** update monthly or mark the data period prominently.
- **Evergreen procurement guides:** review every six months.
- **Product articles:** refresh when grades, packing, availability or evidence changes.
- Redirect retired URLs to the closest valid replacement; do not leave obsolete indexed pages.

## Measurement

Review results monthly in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools:

- non-brand impressions and clicks by guide;
- query-to-page relevance and click-through rate;
- indexed/canonical status and rich-result eligibility;
- engaged sessions and scroll depth;
- quote-form, email and WhatsApp conversions attributed or assisted by content;
- qualified enquiries by commodity and destination;
- earned links or citations from relevant trade sites.

The first meaningful evaluation window is usually 8–12 weeks after discovery, but indexing and ranking timing are controlled by search engines. Improve pages using query and conversion evidence; do not create near-duplicate pages merely to target keyword variants.
