# SEO Implementation Phase 14 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 62–66: de-oiled rice bran, Kabuli chickpeas, whole green mung beans, the green mung buyer guide and toor dal.

## Implemented work

### De-oiled rice bran (DORB)

- Defined DORB as de-oiled or defatted rice bran resulting from rice-bran oil extraction.
- Clarified that DORB is a feed ingredient, not a complete feed or protein guarantee.
- Added powder/pellet form, intended animal species, extraction/process evidence, composition method/basis, acid-insoluble ash terminology, feed safety and batch traceability.
- Replaced fixed protein, fibre, moisture, sand/silica, packing and payload values with contracted lot fields.
- Added a feed-buyer FAQ and links to soybean meal, COA, purchase-specification and sample workflows.

### Kabuli chickpeas

- Identified whole Kabuli chickpeas as *Cicer arietinum* using the Codex pulse standard.
- Removed unverified 7/8/9/12 mm, count and multi-country origin claims.
- Clarified that calibre, sieve millimetres and count-per-weight are different sizing systems and must not be converted without evidence.
- Added retained/passing distribution, sampling, tolerance, defects, crop origin, process status, food safety and lot-linked evidence.

### Whole green mung beans

- Standardized identity around whole green mung/moong, *Vigna radiata*.
- Separated whole green beans from polished, dehulled and split yellow moong forms.
- Added measurable size distribution, defects, crop/origin, food-safety and lot-release fields.
- Separated normal cooking/milling lots from beans intended for sprouting, which require a dedicated pathogen-control programme.
- Added a reciprocal link to the green mung buyer guide.

### Green mung buyer guide

- Replaced unsupported demand, market-ranking and fixed-grade claims with an importer-focused specification guide.
- Added a measurable purchase table for identity, form, crop/origin, size, defects, safety, packing and release evidence.
- Added a dedicated sprouting-use warning and a shipment document-matching workflow.
- Connected the guide to the product page, purchase-specification guide, COA guide and sample request.
- Updated visible and Article structured-data modification dates to 20 August 2026.

### Toor dal

- Consolidated toor dal, tur dal, arhar dal and split pigeon pea on the existing canonical URL.
- Identified the product as dehulled split *Cajanus cajan* and separated it from whole pigeon pea.
- Reframed oily/non-oily as a declared processing or surface-treatment status rather than a complete grade.
- Removed unsupported Africa/Myanmar origin, fixed defect, moisture, packing and payload claims.
- Added crop/processing origin, defects, food safety, sensory/cooking trial and lot-evidence requirements.

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee feed suitability, composition, form, calibre, count, size, crop origin, processing, sprouting suitability, defect limits, food/feed safety results, treatment, packing, payload, document availability or destination compliance. Those outcomes depend on the signed specification, current law, representative sampling, named methods, lot-linked evidence and buyer verification.
