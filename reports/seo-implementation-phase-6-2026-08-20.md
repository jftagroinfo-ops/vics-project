# SEO Implementation Phase 6 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 24–26: the India-versus-Thailand rice comparison, Indian rice import-duty guide, and Nigeria/West Africa rice-import guide.

## Implemented work

### India versus Thailand rice

- Removed unsupported exact prices, country-wide quality rankings and buyer-preference generalizations.
- Reframed the article around official product identity, grade, processing form, broken percentage, crop/lot, sample and quote terms.
- Added a like-for-like quotation checklist and contextual paths to the Basmati, IR64 and purchase-specification guides.
- Cited APEDA's Basmati and non-Basmati profiles and Thailand's official rice standard.

### Indian rice import duty

- Removed fixed country duty rates and the static landed-cost example, which could become inaccurate as tariffs and shipment facts change.
- Explained classification, origin, preference, quota, tax and shipment-date variables.
- Added official lookup routes for the UAE, European Union, Great Britain and Nigeria.
- Replaced the worked example with a reusable formula and seven-step verification workflow.

### Nigeria and West Africa imports

- Removed blanket statements about country grade preferences, universal inspection documents and payment method.
- Added a buyer RFQ matrix, shipment-specific document matrix and contract/counterparty controls.
- Linked Nigeria's official Trade Portal, tariff search and trader guidance, plus NAFDAC's imported-food registration guidance.
- Connected the article to the Nigeria duty guide, documentation and inspection resources, Africa hub, and three relevant product grades.
