# Phase 37 — Digital PR Strategy
**Domain:** https://jftagro.com · **Date:** 2026-08-29

> Strategy only. No outreach executed, no accounts created, no deployment.

## 1. Guiding rules
- **No fabricated news.** Every PR angle must be a REAL event, dataset, or genuine milestone.
- **No paid-placement / link-network PR.** Earned, editorial coverage only.
- Tie to the linkable-assets strategy (publish the asset, then pitch the *resource*, not a link).

## 2. Legitimate PR angles (REAL-data only)
1. **Original commodity research** — publish government-sourced export-indicator report; pitch to trade press and associations.
2. **Genuine export milestone** — if/when a real shipment/contract/certification occurs, issue a factual note (no embellishment).
3. **Useful resource pitch** — offer calculators/guides to buyer-resource curators and industry newsletters.
4. **Expert commentary** — provide sourced commentary on agri-export topics to journalists (no self-promotional spin).

## 3. Outreach posture
- Outreach is **classed as EXTERNAL OUTREACH** and is **NOT AUTHORIZED** in this phase.
- When authorized: target relevant trade/commodity editors and associations only; personalize; lead with the resource's utility.

## 4. Measurement (once authorized)
- Referring domains gained (verified via export, not assumed).
- Mentions in trade press (REAL, attributable).
- Corroboration with Phase 36 AI-search monitoring (`monitor_template.py`).

## 5. Guardrails
- No guarantee of coverage (modeled/inferrred only).
- No acquisition of links from the automated-SEO domains observed in the forensic audit.
