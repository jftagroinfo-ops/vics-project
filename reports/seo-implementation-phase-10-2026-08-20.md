# SEO Implementation Phase 10 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 42–46: turmeric finger, the Salem-versus-Nizamabad turmeric guide, the 2026 turmeric market outlook, dry red chilli and the Teja chilli buyer guide.

## Implemented work

### Turmeric finger

- Replaced fixed 2–5% curcumin, lead-free, aflatoxin-BDL and fixed-purity claims with buyer-defined, lot-specific analytical evidence.
- Distinguished Curcuma longa L. identity and whole finger, bulb, pieces and powder styles.
- Added curcuminoid basis/method, moisture, ash, defective-rhizome, foreign-matter, polish, safety-panel, sample and lot-release fields.
- Connected the product page to Codex CXS 359-2024, the Salem/Nizamabad guide and the purchase-specification workflow.

### Salem versus Nizamabad guide

- Removed universal curcumin ranges, fixed FOB prices, market-by-market applications and unsupported sourcing recommendations.
- Clarified that locality or trade-origin names do not prove analytical results for an offered lot.
- Added a name-neutral evidence table covering traceability, botanical identity, style, dimensions, curcuminoids, defects, polish and safety.
- Linked ICAR-IISR cultivation context to a measurable Codex-based RFQ rather than assigning universal cultivar performance.

### Turmeric market outlook

- Removed unsupported arrival, production, demand, shortage, price-projection and contracting-advice claims.
- Added a dated Spices Board snapshot for Nizamabad finger, Chennai Erode and Chennai Salem for the week ending 1 August 2026.
- Preserved the source report's limitation that its domestic prices are indicative, secondary-source observations and not contractual benchmarks.
- Added a monthly refresh workflow using Spices Board publications and the Government of India AGMARKNET dataset.
- Separated mandi observations from FOB/CFR/CIF quotations and added a formula-only quote structure.

### Dry red chilli and Teja guide

- Replaced fixed variety-wide SHU, ASTA colour, moisture, broken-pod, foreign-matter and aflatoxin values with buyer-defined methods and limits.
- Added separate pungency, colour, physical-defect, destination-safety, approved-sample and lot-release fields.
- Removed fixed FOB benchmarks, universal destination preferences, blanket test mandates and universal document claims.
- Added a comparison table for Teja/S17, Guntur Sannam and Byadagi that separates identity evidence from heat, colour and safety evidence.
- Connected the product and guide to Codex CXS 353-2022, Spices Board GI information, the purchase-specification guide and sample workflow.

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee origin, curcuminoid content, pungency, colour, intended-use suitability, residue acceptance, document availability, price direction, freight or destination approval. Those outcomes depend on traceability, the written specification, current law, named methods, representative sampling, lot evidence and buyer verification.
