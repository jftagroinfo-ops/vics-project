# SEO Implementation Phase 12 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 52–56: nigella seeds, ajwain seeds and powder, natural and hulled sesame, the sesame grade guide, and groundnuts.

## Implemented work

### Nigella seeds

- Reframed the page around *Nigella sativa* and kalonji, with an explicit distinction from culinary cumin (*Cuminum cyminum*).
- Retained “black cumin” only as an ambiguous trade term that must be resolved through botanical identity on the enquiry, sample and contract.
- Replaced fixed purity, moisture, ash, volatile-oil and origin values with physical, composition, safety and lot-evidence fields.
- Linked the page to cumin, purchase-specification and trade-sample routes.

### Ajwain seeds and powder

- Identified ajwain as *Trachyspermum ammi* and noted that the commercial “seed” is botanically the fruit.
- Separated whole ajwain from powder availability and specification requirements.
- Added mesh or particle-size, grinding and treatment status, microbiology, barrier packing and lot-linked evidence for powder.
- Replaced one combined fixed-value table with form-specific buyer fields.

### Natural and hulled sesame

- Made natural, hulled and black sesame separate grade choices rather than interchangeable table entries.
- Defined natural sesame by retained hull and required a hulling-process and treatment declaration for hulled sesame.
- Added residual-hull, wholeness, microbiology, residues, contaminants, sesame-allergen controls, sampling and lot evidence.
- Connected the product page to the sesame guide, groundnuts, purchase-specification and sample workflows.

### Sesame buyer guide

- Added a direct natural-versus-hulled decision table covering hull status, appearance, process and safety evidence.
- Removed the assumption that all hulled sesame is mechanically processed and required the actual hulling/treatment process to be declared.
- Added low-moisture-food Salmonella context and sesame-allergen planning.
- Reworked related links into a product, comparison, purchase-specification and sample enquiry path.
- Updated visible and Article structured-data modification dates to 20 August 2026.

### Groundnuts

- Removed unsupported fixed Bold/Java, count-band, moisture and “aflatoxin negative” claims.
- Added in-shell/kernel identity, measurable count basis, method and tolerance, physical defects and representative-lot evidence.
- Required applicable aflatoxin B1 and/or total-aflatoxin limits, sampling plan, analytical method, laboratory and lot identity.
- Added peanut-allergen, microbiology, packing and shipment-release controls.

## Scope boundary

The pages do not guarantee botanical identity, form availability, grade, count, purity, process, treatment, microbiological results, residue or contaminant acceptance, aflatoxin compliance, document availability, packing approval or destination compliance. Those outcomes depend on the signed specification, current law, representative sampling, named methods, lot-linked evidence and buyer verification.
