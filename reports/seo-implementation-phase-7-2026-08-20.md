# SEO Implementation Phase 7 — 20 August 2026

## Outcome

This phase addressed opportunity-map ranks 27–31: wheat flour, chakki atta, private-label atta, bulk maida and semolina/sooji/rava.

## Implemented work

### Wheat flour, chakki atta and private-label atta

- Retained one canonical URL for the wheat flour and chakki atta synonym cluster to avoid competing pages.
- Reworked the title and H1 around “wheat flour” and “chakki atta exporter India” intent.
- Added a buyer packing matrix covering sales channel, net weight, bag construction, artwork, destination labelling, traceability and container planning.
- Changed private-label language from unconditional availability to an approval-based feasibility review covering volume, packaging minimums, artwork, legal text and production constraints.
- Required separate approval of the flour sample and finished-pack proof.

### Bulk maida

- Replaced generic premium and international-standards language in the search snippet and hero.
- Added buyer decision fields for bakery, noodles, biscuits/confectionery and other processing enquiries.
- Made clear that application references are enquiry paths, not suitability guarantees; the buyer's written specification and production trial control acceptance.
- Added a packing decision path for the published 50 kg PP reference and conditional alternative/private-label formats.
- Replaced unsupported optical-sorting purity and unconditional document language with product-identity and contract-specific controls.

### Semolina, sooji, suji and rava

- Mapped international and Indian synonyms naturally in the title, H1 and explanatory copy without creating another URL.
- Explained that synonyms alone do not identify wheat type, measured granulation or application performance.
- Distinguished semolina (suji/rawa) from durum wheat semolina using current FSSAI terminology.
- Added an RFQ matrix for product identity, sieve distribution, application trial, release tests and packing.
- Replaced unsupported purity and universal-document wording with contract-dependent requirements.

These depend on written specifications, evidence, packaging approval, current policy, destination requirements and the buyer-approved sample or trial.
