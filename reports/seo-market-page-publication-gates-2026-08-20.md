# Market Page Publication Gates — 20 August 2026

## Purpose

This record prevents planned country/product URLs from being published as thin, duplicative or unsupported supplier pages. Passing keyword opportunity review is not enough; operational serviceability and unique evidence are required.

## Nigeria rice market page

**Planned URL:** `/markets/nigeria/rice/`
**Decision:** Hold — not created or added to the sitemap.

Publication requires all of the following:

1. Written confirmation that the exact rice tariff line and proposed sea/import route are currently permitted for the transaction.
2. A named Nigerian importer and confirmed NAFDAC registration, bulk-food or other applicable permit pathway.
3. Approved product forms, specifications, samples, packing, payment review, inspection scope and carrier/logistics serviceability.
4. Unique transactional evidence beyond the existing Nigeria and West Africa buyer guide.
5. Current customs, levy, foreign-exchange and destination-document review at publication and shipment dates.

Current canonical resource: `blog-how-to-import-rice-nigeria-west-africa.html`.

Primary source: NAFDAC imported-food guideline, reviewed 28 February 2026: <https://www.nafdac.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/Files/Resources/Guidelines/FOOD_GUIDELINES/Guidelines-for-Registration-of-Imported-Food-Products-in-Nigeria.pdf>
