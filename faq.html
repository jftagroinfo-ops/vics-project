Ordering & Samples

What is the minimum order quantity for an export order?

The usual starting point for bulk commodities is one full container. The final load quantity depends on product density, bag size, pallet use and destination limits. LCL or air freight may be considered for selected high-value products and trials.

Can I request product samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Qualified buyers can request representative samples for specification and application testing. Sample availability, quantity, courier cost and destination import restrictions are confirmed before dispatch.

Start a sample request with the product, destination and intended application.

Can JFT Agro supply a custom product specification?

Supply can be evaluated against your measurable requirements, including variety, grade, moisture, purity, defects, treatment, microbiology, residues, packing and destination. Feasibility is confirmed before quotation or contract acceptance.

What information is needed for an accurate export quotation?

Provide the product and grade, quantity, destination port, preferred Incoterm, packing, required certificates, inspection needs and target shipment window. A specification or approved sample helps prevent assumptions.

Quality & Inspection

How is product quality agreed before production?

Quality should be defined through a written specification and, where useful, a sealed approved sample. The contract should identify tolerances, test methods, sampling plan, laboratory and inspection responsibility.

Can I arrange independent pre-shipment inspection?

Yes. Independent inspection can be arranged when included in the quotation or contract. Define whether the scope covers quantity, packing, container condition, sampling, laboratory testing, loading supervision or seal verification.

Payment & Documents

What payment terms are available?

Terms are agreed for each contract after reviewing order value, destination, banking route, buyer relationship and risk. Structures may include staged telegraphic transfer or an acceptable irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

Compare the controls in our LC versus TT payment guide.

Which Incoterms can be quoted?

Common requests include EXW or ex-mill, FOB, CFR and CIF. The exact named place or port and the applicable Incoterms edition should appear in the quotation and contract.

Which export documents are supplied with a shipment?

Commercial invoice, packing list and transport documents are standard. Certificates of origin, phytosanitary, fumigation, analysis, inspection or other documents are supplied when required by the product, destination or contract.

The final document list should be agreed before contracting, not after loading.

Packing & Shipping

Can you provide private-label or custom packaging?

Private-label and custom bulk packing can be evaluated subject to product volume, packaging minimums, artwork approval, destination labelling and production feasibility. Grain and finished-pack samples should be approved separately.

Review the complete private-label rice workflow.

How long does an export order take?

Lead time depends on crop availability, processing, testing, packaging, document approvals, vessel schedules and destination requirements. A shipment window is confirmed only after specifications and commercial terms are agreed.

How are shipment and container loading documented?

The agreed shipment file may include packing records, container-condition photographs, loading photographs, seal details, transport documents and inspection reports. The exact evidence package should be written into the contract.

Compliance & Verification

Are phytosanitary and fumigation certificates always required?

Not for every product or destination. Requirements depend on the commodity, processing level, origin, destination and current import rules. Confirm requirements before contracting so the correct certificates and treatments can be planned.

How can buyers verify registrations and certificates?

Request current stamped copies, check the legal entity name and validity dates, and verify registration numbers with the issuing authority where an online service is available. JFT Agro provides relevant current documents for due diligence before contracting.

Essential Trade Glossary

Compact definitions for reading quotations and shipment documents. Always pair the term with the exact named port, place or document requirement.

FOB

Seller delivers the goods on board at the named port; the buyer arranges the main carriage.

CFR

Seller pays cost and freight to the named destination port; cargo insurance is not included by the term.

CIF

CFR obligations plus seller-arranged cargo insurance meeting the applicable Incoterms requirement.

Bill of Lading

Carrier document acknowledging receipt and recording shipment and carriage information.

Certificate of Analysis

Laboratory or quality report showing results for the named sample or lot.

Phytosanitary Certificate

Official plant-health certificate used when the commodity and destination require it.

HS Code

Harmonized System classification used as the basis for customs classification and tariffs.

FCL / LCL

Full Container Load and Less than Container Load describe container-space arrangements.
