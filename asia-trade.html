Compliance

Asia Import Regulations

Halal Evidence

Where halal certification is required, the accepted scheme, product scope, facility and certificate validity are confirmed with the importer before the order is accepted.

Phytosanitary Documentation

Plant-health documents are arranged where required. The importer confirms current permit conditions and any additional declarations before dispatch.

Tariff Preference Review

Any preferential tariff treatment depends on the current HS line and rules of origin. The importer or broker verifies eligibility before contracting.

Residue & Laboratory Scope

Where testing is contracted, destination limits, analyte list, sampling method and nominated laboratory are agreed in writing before loading.
