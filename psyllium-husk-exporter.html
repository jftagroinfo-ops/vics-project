Product-specific FAQ

Psyllium Husk Exporter: buyer questions

What proves a psyllium-husk grade?

A percentage label alone does not. Define what purity means, the test method, swelling method and acceptance limits, then match the sample and COA to the offered lot.

Is whole psyllium husk the same as husk powder?

No. Powder is milled and needs a particle-size or mesh specification with a stated sieve method. Whole husk should not be sold only by a powder mesh label.

Which FSSAI reference values apply?

The official FSSAI notification lists moisture, total ash, acid-insoluble ash, swelling volume and organic extraneous matter criteria. Confirm the current rule and actual lot values before contracting.

Which safety and process declarations should be requested?

Agree microbiological, pesticide-residue and heavy-metal testing for the destination, plus treatment or sterilisation status, ethylene-oxide status where relevant, and allergen/cross-contact information.

Can buyers review a sample and COA?

Yes. The sample identity, lot identity, laboratory, method and report date should be traceable so results can be compared before the purchase order is signed.
