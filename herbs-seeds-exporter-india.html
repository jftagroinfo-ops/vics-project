Overview

What JFT Agro Supplies

JFT Agro Overseas exports 15 herb and functional-seed products: senna leaves and pods, asaliya seeds, black himaj, basil seeds, cassia tora seeds and meal, galangal root, akarkara root, moringa leaves and powder, psyllium husk, henna leaves and powder, and wheat grass powder. These products serve pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, cosmetic and industrial buyers more often than food-retail buyers, which shapes the specification and documentation each one needs.

Specification & Selection Considerations

Herb and functional-seed buyers typically specify active-content or purity thresholds relevant to their end use (for example, sennoside content for senna, or swelling factor for psyllium husk) rather than the culinary-grade metrics used for spices. Each product page states its own reference specification; 10 distinct HS codes apply across this group.

This group's most frequently documented markets are the USA, Europe, the Middle East, China, Japan and Vietnam.
