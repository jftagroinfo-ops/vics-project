EU & UK Compliance Controls
We Plan Per Shipment

The buyer's specification and destination rules define the required controls. The following items are scoped and documented before shipment:

Pesticide MRL Scope

The destination limits, analyte list, sampling method and laboratory are agreed before testing. Results are assessed against the contracted specification, not a generic standard.

Management & Food Safety

Current supplier and facility certificates relevant to the order are shared for buyer review. Certification scope and validity should be checked before contracting.

Organic Orders

Organic status is never assumed. The approved product, operator scope, transaction documents and destination recognition are verified before an organic offer is issued.

Basmati Identity & Traceability

Variety, origin and traceability evidence are agreed to the buyer's import and retail requirements. Additional authenticity testing can be included in the contract.
