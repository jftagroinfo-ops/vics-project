Intended applicationFood ingredient, supplement input or cosmetic raw material stated before testing

Product-specific FAQ

Moringa Powder: commercial questions

What specification is published for Moringa Powder?
The current reference specification lists Mesh Size: 80-100 Mesh; Color: Bright Green; Moisture: 8% Max; Ash: 10% Max; Additives: None. These are planning values; the signed specification, approved sample and agreed tolerances control the order.
What are the HS code, MOQ and packing options for Moringa Powder?
Reference HS code: 07129090. MOQ: 1 FCL (20ft). Published packing: 25kg Drum/Bag. Classification, packing and legal weight limits must be confirmed for the destination and contract.
How much Moringa Powder fits in a container?
The planning reference for a 20-foot container is 14 MT in 20ft. Final payload depends on bag construction, palletisation, carrier limits, route and applicable law; the written loading plan controls.
Which documents can be requested for Moringa Powder?
The base document set is Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Transport document, Certificate of Origin when applicable. Phytosanitary, Fumigation, COA, Inspection report, Health or destination-specific certificate are arranged only when applicable and written into the quotation or contract.
Can I request a trade sample of Moringa Powder?
Qualified trade buyers may request a standard 250–500g evaluation sample per selected product. Availability, final size, courier cost and dispatch timing are confirmed before shipment; larger samples require separate written approval.
