Product-specific FAQ

Indian White Sugar S30 Supplier: buyer questions

What does S30 mean for Indian white sugar?

S30 is a commercial granulation description, not a Codex category. A purchase specification should state sieve apertures, percentages passing or retained, sampling method and tolerance.

Which quality values should be lot-tested?

Agree methods and limits for polarization, colour/ICUMSA, moisture, conductivity ash and reducing sugars. Website examples do not replace the offered lot’s COA or an agreed inspection report.

What is the current Indian sugar export-policy position?

As checked on 20 August 2026, the DGFT Schedule-2 notification lists relevant raw, white, refined and organic sugar lines as restricted until further orders, subject to specific permission and stated quota exceptions. Verify the latest DGFT and Department of Food & Public Distribution instructions before every quotation and shipment.

Can supply be promised before export permission is confirmed?

No unconditional export-availability claim should be inferred. Commercial commitment depends on current policy, product classification, permission where required, allocation or exception if applicable, and destination compliance.

How are packing and payload confirmed?

Bag construction, liner, net weight, markings, palletisation, carrier limits and legal payload are stated in the written packing and loading plan.
