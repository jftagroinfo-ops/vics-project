Transparent Flow

How Every Order
Gets Executed

01

Inquiry & Proforma

Complete requirements are reviewed before a Proforma Invoice is prepared, including the applicable CIF/FOB breakdown.

02

Processing & Milling

Processing, grading, optical sorting and moisture controls are confirmed for the contracted product and documented during buyer due diligence.

03

Inspection and Testing

The agreed laboratory tests or pre-shipment inspection are arranged when required. Certificates are supplied according to the product, destination and contract.

04

Container and Port Handover

Container condition, stuffing plan, transport and port handover are coordinated against the confirmed booking.

05

Documents and Dispatch

The invoice, packing list, transport document, origin certificate and any product-specific documents are prepared as agreed.

Agree the Checks
Before Production

Each order follows the testing and inspection scope agreed in the Proforma Invoice. Independent laboratory or pre-shipment inspection can be included, and reference-sample retention is confirmed per contract.

Documented Acceptance — Approved samples, tolerances and inspection scope are recorded before dispatch.

Shipment Documentation — Applicable registrations, origin records and export documents are supplied as agreed for the destination.

Verify Before You
Commit to a Shipment

We encourage importers to validate the company, approve a representative sample, confirm contracted specifications and agree the inspection and document scope before payment.

1. Verify registrations

Request current stamped registration and certification copies relevant to the product.

2. Approve the sample

Record the agreed grade, tolerance, packing and lab parameters before production.

3. Confirm inspection

Add SGS, Intertek or another independent inspection to the order when required.

4. Match the documents

Check the Proforma Invoice, payment terms and destination document list before remittance.
