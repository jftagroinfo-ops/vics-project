# Website Claims Governance

Public commercial claims must be supportable, current, scoped and owned. This file is the release gate for factual claims that are stronger than ordinary product descriptions.

The working register is `docs/claim-verification-matrix.csv`. `PENDING_VERIFICATION` is not permission to publish an unqualified assertion. Evidence owners must complete every applicable field and change the status to `APPROVED` before restoring stronger wording.

## Status definitions

- `PENDING_VERIFICATION`: evidence has not been recorded; publish only the approved neutral wording.
- `CONDITIONAL`: the statement is true only for a named order, product, facility, provider or contract.
- `REJECTED_PENDING_SCOPE`: the wording misstates ownership/accreditation or lacks the required scope.
- `REJECTED_AS_GUARANTEE`: the absolute claim is not suitable for publication.
- `APPROVED`: evidence, scope, owner and review/expiry date are recorded and the exact public wording has been signed off.

## Publication rule

A claim may be published only when the evidence owner records:

- exact public wording;
- source document or system;
- legal entity and facility/product scope;
- effective and expiry/review dates;
- reviewer name/role;
- whether a redacted public copy may be shown.

Expired or unverified claims must be removed or changed to neutral wording such as “current copy available on request,” “subject to contract,” or “confirmed per booking.”

## Claims requiring current evidence

| Claim family | Required evidence | Review frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Star Export House / AEO / APEDA / FSSAI / ISO / HACCP | Current certificate or registration showing holder, scope, issuer and dates | Before expiry and quarterly |
| Milling or packing capacity | Facility record and responsible operations approval | Quarterly |
| Countries served / shipment or container volume | Reconciled shipment records and defined measurement period | Quarterly |
| Carrier agreements or space commitments | Current executed carrier/forwarder agreement and permitted public wording | Monthly |
| Stock or availability | Current inventory/production confirmation | Per enquiry; do not publish as evergreen stock |
| Testing and inspection | Contracted specification, laboratory/inspection scope and batch/shipment record | Per shipment |
| Response, transit or customs time | Measured service data and clear conditions; never guarantee authority/carrier outcomes | Monthly |
| Market prices, production or demand | Named authoritative source and “data as of” date | At least monthly |

## Release check

The content owner must review homepage, certificates, infrastructure, product templates, calculators, regional pages, articles and translations. Native-language reviewers must approve translated commercial and legal meaning—not only grammar.
