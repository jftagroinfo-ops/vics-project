Overview

What JFT Agro Supplies

JFT Agro Overseas exports 15 animal-feed input products: yellow maize (corn), white sorghum (jowar), green millet (bajra), grey millet, red millet, safflower seeds, sunflower seeds, maize grits, soybean meal (DOC), deoiled rice bran (DORB), groundnut oil cake, barley seeds, corn gluten meal, cotton seed oil cake and buckwheat. Feed-input buyers specify by protein/oil/fibre content and admixture limits relevant to compound-feed formulation, which differs from the food-grade specifications used for the same grains sold for human consumption.

Specification & Selection Considerations

Protein content (for meals and oil cakes), oil/fat content, fibre, moisture and admixture are the primary specification fields buyers request for feed-input use. 14 distinct HS codes apply across this group -- confirm the correct code for the intended feed-vs-food use before quoting.

This group's most frequently documented markets are Vietnam, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, Bangladesh and China.
