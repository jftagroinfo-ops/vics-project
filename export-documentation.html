Importer reference

Agro Export Documents for India Shipments

Build a shipment-specific document matrix for Indian rice, spices, pulses, grains and other agricultural products. The exact set depends on commodity, origin, destination, Incoterm, payment method and contract.

Core shipment documents

These summaries explain purpose and responsibility; they do not promise that every document applies to every shipment.

Commercial Invoice

What
Seller’s commercial record of goods, value, currency and terms.
Who
Issued by the exporter.
Check
Entity names, description, HS code, quantity, Incoterm and payment terms.

Packing List

What
Package count, net/gross weight, marks and dimensions.
Who
Issued by the exporter/packer.
Check
Totals must reconcile with invoice and transport document.

Bill of Lading

What
Carrier transport document and, depending on form, document of title.
Who
Issued by the carrier or authorised agent.
Check
Shipper, consignee, notify party, ports, packages and release method.

Certificate of Origin

What
Evidence of declared country of origin; preferential forms require eligibility.
Who
Authorised chamber/agency or other permitted issuer.
Check
Destination scheme, origin rule, invoice and product match.

Phytosanitary Certificate

What
Plant-health certification where required by the importing country.
Who
Indian plant quarantine authority.
Check
Import permit, additional declarations, treatment and inspection timing.

Fumigation Certificate

What
Treatment details for goods or wood packaging when required.
Who
Approved treatment provider under the applicable rules.
Check
Chemical, dosage, duration, temperature, lot/container and destination rules.

Certificate of Analysis

What
Results for the agreed physical, chemical or microbiological parameters.
Who
Named laboratory or quality party agreed in the contract.
Check
Sample/lot identity, methods, accreditation scope and acceptance limits.

Inspection Certificate

What
Independent findings on contracted quality, quantity, weight or packing scope.
Who
Inspection company appointed under the contract.
Check
Appointment, scope, sampling, exclusions and report recipient.

Insurance Certificate

What
Evidence of marine cargo cover where seller-arranged insurance applies.
Who
Insurer or authorised intermediary.
Check
Assured party, voyage, value, risks, exclusions, claims contact and endorsement.
Document sequence

Build the Matrix by Shipment Stage

1. Supplier approval

Legal identity, export registration, facility evidence, licence and certification scope. These establish the counterparty; they do not replace shipment documents.

2. Before production

Approved specification, import permit conditions, artwork, test plan, inspection instruction and document wording.

3. Before loading

Lot COA, inspection/weight record, treatment evidence, packing reconciliation, container condition and draft commercial set.

4. After departure

Final invoice, packing list, transport document, origin and required regulatory certificates sent through the contract or bank route.

Responsibility controls

Document Trigger, Issuer and Deadline

Do not request every certificate for every shipment. Record the legal, permit, contract or bank trigger and confirm who can validly issue the evidence.

QuestionRecord in the matrixCommon failure to prevent
Why is it required?Destination law, import permit, contract, Incoterm or bank/LC clauseBuying unnecessary evidence or omitting a mandatory certificate
Who issues it?Exporter, carrier, chamber, government authority, laboratory, inspector or insurerUsing a private declaration where an official certificate is required
What must match?Entities, product description, HS line, lot, packages, weight, ports, dates and marksCross-document discrepancies that delay clearance or bank presentation
When is it due?Before production, loading, customs filing, vessel departure or bank presentationDiscovering wording or inspection requirements after cargo is loaded
What format?Electronic/original/copy count, language, legalization and endorsement requirementsCorrect content supplied in an unacceptable form
Destination rules change. Health certificates, halal documents, import permits, conformity certificates, sanctions screening and laboratory parameters may be destination- or buyer-specific. The signed contract should state who obtains each document and who bears the cost.
