Teja Red Chilli Exporter India: Buyer Specification Guide

Teja S17, Guntur Sannam and Byadagi are useful selection terms, but the names alone do not prove heat, colour, defects, origin entitlement or destination compliance for a particular lot.

The Spices Board GI list for spices includes Guntur Sannam Chilli and Byadagi Chilli. GI or origin evidence supports identity; it does not replace heat, colour or safety testing for the shipment.

Measure Pungency and Colour Separately

Codex CXS 353-2022 covers dried or dehydrated chilli pepper and paprika and provides identity, style, chemical and physical fields. The RFQ should name the analytical methods and sampling plan rather than relying on a variety-wide SHU or colour range.

  • Pungency: acceptable SHU range, method, sample preparation, units and tolerance
  • Colour: required colour value, method, form tested and tolerance
  • Physical quality: moisture, stalks, broken pods, loose seeds, foreign matter, mould and insect damage
  • Presentation: whole/ground form, pod dimensions where relevant and approved sealed sample

Destination Safety Panel

Define the pesticide-residue, mycotoxin, unauthorised-colour and microbiological panels for the destination and intended use. Do not state that one test or certificate is universally mandatory or sufficient. For EU shipments, verify current pesticide limits in the EU Pesticides Database; other destinations require their own current checks.

The contract should identify analytes, limits, methods, laboratory status, sampling responsibility, lot code, report timing and the action if a result fails.

Packing and Shipment Evidence

  • Bag or carton construction, liner, net weight and legal markings
  • Moisture protection, container cleanliness and odour controls
  • Loading plan, lot segregation, seal record and retained samples
  • Commercial and destination-specific documents listed individually in the contract

Teja Chilli RFQ Checklist

Send identity evidence, whole or ground form, SHU and colour requirements with methods, physical tolerances, destination safety panel, packing, quantity, Incoterm, named place or port, shipment window and quote validity. Link the RFQ to the dry red chilli exporter page, structure it with the purchase specification guide, and approve a representative trade sample.

Buyer rule: Do not purchase a variety name plus an assumed heat or colour range. Purchase a documented identity, measurable specification, approved sample and lot-release process.
