How to Read an Indian Export Proforma Invoice: Complete Guide

A proforma invoice is the first step in every international food trade. This guide helps you decode the important fields, verify supplier commitments, and spot pricing or specification issues before the order is placed.

Why Proforma Invoices Matter

A proforma invoice sets expectations for price, quantity, packaging, and payment terms. For Indian food exports, it also forms the basis for customs clearance, bank approvals, and logistics planning.

Key Sections to Review

Focus on the product description, HS codes, unit price, total amount, packaging details, shipment terms, and delivery port. Ensure the weight, grade, and quality definitions match your purchase order.

Must-check invoice items

  • Seller and buyer contact details
  • Product name, grade, and specification
  • Qty in MT or bags, and unit conversion details
  • FOB/CIF term, loading and discharge ports
  • Payment method and validity period

Common Proforma Invoice Mistakes

Watch for unclear product descriptions, missing HS codes, absent packing details, or open-ended payment instructions. These can cause delays in LC issuance, customs, and cargo release.

Tip: Ask the supplier to attach a detailed packing list and product specification sheet so the proforma invoice can be compared against the actual shipment.

What Comes After the Proforma Invoice

After approving the proforma invoice, importers usually open an LC or make an advance payment. The proforma also guides the commercial invoice and export documents prepared at the time of shipment.
